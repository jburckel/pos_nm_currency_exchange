=====================
POS Currency Exchange
=====================

Currency exchange counter inside the Odoo 20 Point of Sale: buy and sell
foreign notes with a commission, on the cash drawers controlled by
`POS Multi-Currency Cash <https://github.com/jburckel/pos_nm_multicurrencies>`_.

Status
======

Skeleton: the module installs and adds nothing yet. The first version will
bring the buy and sell operation, the commission per currency, the exchange
receipt and the entries in the closing control, the Currency Position report
and the session report.

Dependencies
============

* ``point_of_sale``
* ``pos_nm_multicurrencies`` (20.0)
