Key Features

One Popup, One Operation

A Currency Exchange entry in the menu of the register. The cashier picks the currency received and the currency handed back, types the amount, and the quote appears: the notes to hand back, the rate applied and the commission. One tap swaps the two currencies. Any two cash currencies of the point of sale can be exchanged, your own included.

Commissions per Currency

A percentage, a fixed part and a minimum, set on each cash currency of the point of sale. The notes handed back follow the change rate and the change rounding of their currency: the customer leaves with whole notes and the rounding stays in the commission.

A Receipt for the Customer

Every operation prints an exchange receipt on the receipt printer of the point of sale: reference, cashier, notes received, notes handed back, rate, commission and the note typed by the cashier, with the header and footer of your company.

Drawers That Stay Right

Each operation posts two lines on the cash journal of the session, in the currency of each side: the drawer of every currency is updated, and the closing control of POS Multi-Currency Cash lists the operations under the cash moves of each currency. The commission lands on a dedicated income account.

Know Your Exchange Business

Every Operation on Record

Point of Sale > Currency Exchanges lists every operation with its point of sale, session, cashier, customer, notes received, notes handed back and commission. Each record links to its two statement lines.

Commissions at a Glance

A pivot of the commissions and values received, per currency, per day, per point of sale or per cashier. The session form carries the number of operations and the commissions of the session.

In the Session Report

The Sales Details report gains a Currency Exchange block: the operations of the period with their amounts and commissions, and the total of the commissions.

Two Settings per Point of Sale

Enable the counter, pick the income account of the commissions and, if your regulation requires it, the value above which the customer must be identified: the register then asks for a customer before confirming the operation.

How It Works

  1. Configure: install POS Multi-Currency Cash, list the currencies on the cash payment method, set the commissions on each cash currency and the commission account on the point of sale.
  2. Exchange: at the register, menu > Currency Exchange, currencies, amount, customer if required, Confirm. The receipt prints.
  3. Close: the closing control shows the notes received and handed back in each drawer; the commission is already on its income account.
  4. Review: Currency Exchanges, the pivot of the commissions, the Currency Position report and the session report.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need POS Multi-Currency Cash?

Yes. The exchange counter works on the drawers, cash rates, coins and notes and closing control of that module. Both modules are available from the same author; ask for a test database with both installed.

Which rates are applied?

The cash rates of POS Multi-Currency Cash: the rate of Odoo with the margin of the point of sale, or the fixed cash rates you set. The notes received are valued at the cash rate of their currency, the notes handed back at the change rate of theirs.

Where does the commission go?

On the income account chosen on the point of sale, through the two statement lines of the operation on the cash journal of the session. Nothing to post by hand.

Can I require the identity of the customer?

Yes. Set the value above which a customer is required: the register refuses to confirm an operation above it without a customer, and the customer is stored on the operation.
