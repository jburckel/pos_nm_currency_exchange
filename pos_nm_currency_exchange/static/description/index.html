POS Currency Exchange

Buy and sell foreign notes at your Point of Sale

A currency exchange counter inside the Odoo Point of Sale, built on the drawers, cash rates and coins and notes of POS Multi-Currency Cash.

Technical Requirements

  • Odoo Version: 20.0
  • Dependencies: Point of Sale (point_of_sale), POS Multi-Currency Cash (pos_nm_multicurrencies)
