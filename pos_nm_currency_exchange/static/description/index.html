Key Features

One Popup, One Operation

A Currency Exchange entry in the menu of the register. The cashier picks the currency received and the currency handed back, types the amount, and the quote appears: the notes to hand back, the rate applied, the commission and what the drawer holds. The customer wants an exact amount? Type it in Handed back: the amount to receive is computed. Any two cash currencies of the point of sale can be exchanged, your own included.

Buy Rate, Sell Rate, Commission

The notes received are bought at the cash rate of POS Multi-Currency Cash, the notes handed back are sold at a sell rate with its own margin: the spread is always in your favour. On top, a commission per currency: a percentage, a fixed part and a minimum. The notes handed back are rounded down to the coins and notes of their currency, your own currency included: the customer leaves with money the drawer really holds, and the rounding stays in the commission.

A Receipt for the Customer

Every operation prints an exchange receipt on the receipt printer of the point of sale: the header and footer of the point of sale, your exchange license number, purchase or sale of foreign currency, reference, date and time, cashier, customer and, when recorded, the type of identity document with the last 4 characters of its number, notes received, notes handed back, rate applied, commission and note. Reprint it from the register (marked COPY), print it in PDF or send it by email from the back office.

Drawers That Stay Right

Each operation posts two lines on the cash journal of the session, in the currency of each side: the drawer of every currency is updated, and the closing control of POS Multi-Currency Cash lists the operations under the cash moves of each currency, plus a Currency Exchange block with the number of operations, each operation, the commissions, the rate margin and the cancelled operations. The register refuses to hand back more than the drawer holds, in every currency. The commission lands on a dedicated income account.

Your Rate Board

An Exchange Rates entry of the register shows, per currency, what you buy and sell 1 unit for, what the customer receives or pays for a typical amount, and the commission, computed exactly like the operations. Print it on the receipt printer for the counter, or in PDF from the back office.

Customer Identification

Require a customer above a value, or for every operation, and an identity document above another value. Operations of the same customer over a period of days can be added up. The document is entered at the register and refused when expired (a document without expiry date is accepted with No expiry date ticked). The full document is kept on the operation and visible to POS Administrators only; at the register the cashier sees its type, issuing country, expiry date and the last 4 characters of its number, and the receipt prints its type and those 4 characters. It helps you apply the identification rules of your country.

The Rate Board on Paper

Print the rate board in PDF for the window of the counter, from the settings or the Print menu of the point of sale: the same lines as the register, with the typical amount rounded to the coins the counter takes and hands back.

Mistakes Undone Cleanly

A POS Administrator cancels an operation while the session is open and the drawer still holds the notes received: two opposite lines put both drawers and the commission account back where they were. Operations cannot be deleted nor edited, except their note by a POS Administrator; cashiers only read them, and the references follow each other without gap.

Your Margin, Measured

Every operation shows its commission and its rate margin, what your rates earn against the rates of Odoo of the day: together, the income of the operation. Both appear in the list, the pivot, the closing control and the Sales Details report.

Know Your Exchange Business

Every Operation on Record

Point of Sale → Orders → Currency Exchanges (POS Administrators) lists every operation with its point of sale, session, cashier, customer, notes received, notes handed back, commission, rate margin and status. Each record links to its statement lines, and keeps a chatter of its changes.

Commissions at a Glance

A pivot of the commissions, rate margins and values received, per day, status and currency received, which you can regroup per point of sale, session or cashier. The session form carries the number of operations and the commissions of the session.

In the Session Report

The Sales Details report gains a Currency Exchange block: the operations of the session, or of the period on the selected points of sale, with their amounts, commissions and rate margins, and the totals per currency.

The Exchange Register

Point of Sale → Reporting → Exchange Register (POS Administrators) lists every operation in the order of its reference, cancelled ones included, with the customer and the identity document. Export it to Excel, or print it in PDF for a period with the totals per currency.

Settings per Point of Sale

Enable the counter, pick the income account of the commissions, print your exchange license number on the receipts and set the identification rules: the register then asks for a customer, an identity document and the origin of the funds before confirming the operation. Link the counter to its exchange office, track its notes by denomination and show the rate board on its customer display.

From One Counter to an Exchange Office

One Office, Several Counters

Group the counters of one licensed place of business in an exchange office: they share its license, its address, its compliance officer and its identification rules. Apply Rates to the Counters copies the rates, roundings, sell margin and commissions of a reference counter to the others, and Rate Differences shows what still differs. One counter only? The office is optional.

Every Drawer of the Office

The Drawers page of the office shows, for each counter with an open session, the cash expected in every currency, the operations and commissions of the day, and the notes in transit between the counters with their total value.

Your Rates on a Screen

A full-screen rate board for a TV in the window, a tablet or the customer display of Odoo: what you buy and sell each currency for, what the customer receives or pays for a typical amount, the commission and your license. It reloads the rates by itself and keeps the last rates on screen when the connection drops. One page per counter, and one per office.

Notes from Counter to Counter

Send Notes and Receive Notes in the menu of the register move notes between the counters of an office, through a notes in transit account: a cash out at the sending counter, a cash in at the receiving one, a slip printed each time and references without gap. A POS Administrator follows them in Point of Sale → Orders → Note Transfers.

Compliance and Notes, Under Control

Compliance, Built In

Above a threshold, the customer declares the origin of the funds, the purpose of the operation and whether he is a politically exposed person. Above another, the operation is flagged To Review, never refused, and the compliance officer gets an activity. Every operation is chained into an inalterable register (SHA-256 hash chain) that you check with Check Integrity and close per period with Close Period.

Notes Counted by Denomination

Tick Track the notes by denomination: the cashier enters the coins and notes received, and the notes handed back are proposed from the stock of the drawer, the largest first. The totals must match the amounts. A drawer short of a note only gets a warning, and the receipt lists the notes.

The Stock of Every Drawer

The Note Stock entry of the register shows, for each foreign coin and note, the opening count, the notes moved by the exchanges and the transfers, and what the drawer should hold. The closing popup gives the expected quantity of each coin and note when the opening was counted by coins and notes, and the Note Stock report compares the stock with the closing count, session by session.

How It Works

  1. Configure: install POS Multi-Currency Cash, list the currencies on the cash payment method, set the sell margin and the commissions on each cash currency, the commission account and the identification rules on the point of sale. With several counters, group them in an exchange office and apply the rates of a reference counter to the others.
  2. Exchange: at the register, menu → Currency Exchange, currencies, amount received or handed back, customer, identity document and declarations if required, notes by denomination if tracked, Confirm. The receipt prints.
  3. Move the notes: Send Notes at one counter, Receive Notes at the other; the notes in transit stay on their own account.
  4. Correct: a POS Administrator cancels a mistaken operation while the session is open and the drawer still holds the notes received.
  5. Close: the closing control lists the notes received and handed back in the cash moves of each drawer, and sums the operations up in a Currency Exchange block; the commission is already on its income account.
  6. Review: Currency Exchanges and its To Review filter, the pivot of the commissions and rate margins, the Exchange Register, its integrity check and its closings, the Note Stock report, the Drawers page of the office, the Currency Position report and the Sales Details report.

Which rates are applied?

The notes received are bought at the cash rate of POS Multi-Currency Cash: the rate of Odoo with its margin, or the fixed rate you set. The notes handed back are sold at the rate of Odoo divided by 1 + the exchange sell margin of their currency, or with fixed rates at the lower of the fixed rate and the fixed change rate. Then the commission of the currency applies.

Where does the commission go?

On the income account chosen on the point of sale, through the two statement lines of the operation on the cash journal of the session. Nothing to post by hand.

Can I require the identity of the customer?

Yes. Set the value above which a customer is required, or require one for every operation, and the value above which an identity document is recorded; operations of the same customer over a period can be added up. The register refuses to confirm without them, and the server checks again. These settings help you apply the rules of your country; they do not replace your own legal review.

How do I correct a mistaken operation?

A POS Administrator opens it and presses Cancel Operation while the session is open and the drawer still holds the notes received: two opposite lines give the customer the notes back and take back the notes handed back, and the commission is reversed. Operations cannot be deleted, so the register stays complete.

Can my cashiers see or change the operations?

Cashiers record operations at the register, reprint the receipts of the session, show the rate board, see the note stock of their drawer and send or receive notes between the counters of their office. The Currency Exchanges and Exchange Register menus, the full identity documents, the declarations and the compliance review, the note of an operation, the cancellation, the exchange offices, the note transfers and the register closings are for POS Administrators; at the register the cashier only sees the type, issuing country, expiry date and last 4 characters of the document of the selected customer.

Does it work with one counter only?

Yes. Exchange offices are optional: a point of sale outside any office keeps its own license and identification rules in its settings. Create an office when several counters of one place of business should share the license, the rules and the rates, or move notes between them.

What does the hash chain of the register guarantee?

Every operation, and its cancellation, is hashed with the hash of the previous operation of the company. Odoo refuses to change the hashed fields of an operation (only its note and its compliance review can change), and Check Integrity finds the first operation changed or removed directly in the database. The chain shows a change; it does not prevent it, and it cannot show that the last operations were removed: keep the register closings, which freeze the hash of the last operation of each period with the PDF register. It helps you keep a reliable register; it is not a certification.

Why is a stock shortfall only a warning?

The stock by denomination follows the exchange operations and the note transfers, but the cash sales and cash moves in a foreign currency change the drawer without denominations. The stock can therefore be behind the real drawer: the popup names the notes it lacks, the cashier checks the drawer, and the next count by coins and notes corrects the stock. An amount that the coins and notes cannot make up is an error, and the notes must add up to the amounts.

Can I show the rates on a screen for the customers?

Yes. Each counter and each office has a full-screen rate board page for a TV, a tablet or any browser, protected by a secret address. It reloads the rates by itself. Tick Rate board on the customer display to show it on the customer display of Odoo while no order is in progress.
