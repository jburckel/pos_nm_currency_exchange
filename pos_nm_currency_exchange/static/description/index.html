POS Currency Exchange

Buy and sell foreign notes at your Point of Sale

A currency exchange counter inside the Odoo Point of Sale: the customer brings notes in one currency and leaves with another, without buying a product. Built on the drawers, cash rates and coins and notes of POS Multi-Currency Cash.

Key Features

One Popup, One Operation

A Currency Exchange entry in the menu of the register. The cashier picks the currency received and the currency handed back, types the amount, and the quote appears: the notes to hand back, the rate applied, the commission and what the drawer holds. The customer wants an exact amount? Type it in Handed back: the amount to receive is computed. Any two cash currencies of the point of sale can be exchanged, your own included.

Buy Rate, Sell Rate, Commission

The notes received are bought at the cash rate of POS Multi-Currency Cash, the notes handed back are sold at a sell rate with its own margin: the spread is always in your favour. On top, a commission per currency: a percentage, a fixed part and a minimum. The notes handed back are rounded down to the coins and notes of their currency, your own currency included: the customer leaves with money the drawer really holds, and the rounding stays in the commission.

A Receipt for the Customer

Every operation prints an exchange receipt on the receipt printer of the point of sale: the header and footer of the point of sale, your exchange licence number, purchase or sale of foreign currency, reference, date and time, cashier, customer, notes received, notes handed back, rate applied and commission. Reprint it from the register (marked COPY), print it in PDF or send it by e-mail from the back office.

Drawers That Stay Right

Each operation posts two lines on the cash journal of the session, in the currency of each side: the drawer of every currency is updated, and the closing control of POS Multi-Currency Cash lists the operations under the cash moves of each currency. The register refuses to hand back more than the drawer holds, in every currency. The commission lands on a dedicated income account.

Your Rate Board

An Exchange Rates entry of the register shows, per currency, what you buy and sell 1 unit for, what the customer receives or pays for a typical amount, and the commission, computed exactly like the operations. Print it on the receipt printer for the counter, or in PDF from the back office.

Customer Identification

Require a customer above a value, or for every operation, and an identity document above another value. Operations of the same customer over a period of days can be added up. The document is entered at the register, refused when expired, kept on the operation and visible to managers only: it helps you apply the identification rules of your country.

Mistakes Undone Cleanly

A manager cancels an operation while the session is open: two opposite lines put both drawers and the commission account back where they were. Operations cannot be edited or deleted, cashiers only read them, and the references follow each other without gap.

Your Margin, Measured

Every operation shows its commission and its rate margin, what your rates earn against the rates of Odoo of the day: together, the income of the operation. Both appear in the list, the pivot, the closing control and the Sales Details report.

Know Your Exchange Business

Every Operation on Record

Point of Sale > Orders > Currency Exchanges (POS Administrators) lists every operation with its point of sale, session, cashier, customer, notes received, notes handed back, commission, rate margin and status. Each record links to its statement lines, and keeps a chatter of its changes.

Commissions at a Glance

A pivot of the commissions, rate margins and values received, per currency, per day, per point of sale or per cashier. The session form carries the number of operations and the commissions of the session.

In the Session Report

The Sales Details report gains a Currency Exchange block: the operations of the session, or of the period on the selected points of sale, with their amounts, commissions and rate margins, and the totals per currency.

The Exchange Register

Point of Sale > Reporting > Exchange Register lists every operation in the order of its reference, cancelled ones included, with the customer and the identity document. Export it to Excel, or print it in PDF for a period with the totals per currency.

Settings per Point of Sale

Enable the counter, pick the income account of the commissions, print your exchange licence number on the receipts and set the identification rules: the register then asks for a customer, and for an identity document, before confirming the operation.

How It Works

  1. Configure: install POS Multi-Currency Cash, list the currencies on the cash payment method, set the sell margin and the commissions on each cash currency, the commission account and the identification rules on the point of sale.
  2. Exchange: at the register, menu > Currency Exchange, currencies, amount received or handed back, customer and identity document if required, Confirm. The receipt prints.
  3. Correct: a manager cancels a mistaken operation while the session is open.
  4. Close: the closing control shows the notes received and handed back in each drawer; the commission is already on its income account.
  5. Review: Currency Exchanges, the pivot of the commissions and rate margins, the Exchange Register, the Currency Position report and the Sales Details report.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need POS Multi-Currency Cash?

Yes, version 20.0.2.1.0 or later. The exchange counter works on the drawers, cash rates, coins and notes and closing control of that module. Both modules are available from the same author; ask for a test database with both installed.

Which rates are applied?

The notes received are bought at the cash rate of POS Multi-Currency Cash: the rate of Odoo with its margin, or the fixed rate you set. The notes handed back are sold at the rate of Odoo less the exchange sell margin of their currency, or with fixed rates at the lower of the fixed rate and the fixed change rate. Then the commission of the currency applies.

Where does the commission go?

On the income account chosen on the point of sale, through the two statement lines of the operation on the cash journal of the session. Nothing to post by hand.

Can I require the identity of the customer?

Yes. Set the value above which a customer is required, or require one for every operation, and the value above which an identity document is recorded; operations of the same customer over a period can be added up. The register refuses to confirm without them, and the server checks again. These settings help you apply the rules of your country; they do not replace your own legal review.

How do I correct a mistaken operation?

A manager opens it and presses Cancel Operation while the session is open: two opposite lines give the customer the notes back and take back the notes handed back, and the commission is reversed. Operations cannot be deleted, so the register stays complete.

Can my cashiers see or change the operations?

Cashiers record operations at the register, reprint the receipts of the session and show the rate board. The Currency Exchanges and Exchange Register menus, the identity documents and the cancellation are for POS Administrators.
