=====================
POS Currency Exchange
=====================

Currency exchange counter inside the Odoo 20 Point of Sale, built on
POS Multi-Currency Cash.

Overview
========

This guide will describe the buy and sell operation, the commissions, the
exchange receipt and the accounting of the operations once the first
version is released.

Requirements
============

* The **Point of Sale** application (``point_of_sale``), Odoo 20.
* **POS Multi-Currency Cash** (``pos_nm_multicurrencies``) 20.0, with at
  least one foreign currency on the cash payment method of the point of
  sale.
